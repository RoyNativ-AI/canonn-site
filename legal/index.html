Privacy Policy
Applies to the Canonn API (api.canonn.ai), canonn.ai, and the Canonn dashboard.

1. Data we collect

CategoryWhat it is, and why
Account dataYour email and authentication identity, handled by our identity provider. We never receive or store your password.
Usage metadataPer request: timestamp, key name, model, token counts, latency, status, finish reason, request id, IP address and user agent. This powers your dashboard and your bill.

2. How we use it

  • To operate the API: routing, authentication, and returning your answers.
  • To meter usage and bill you accurately.
  • To detect abuse, fraud and attacks against the service.
  • To show you your own usage and logs in the dashboard.
  • To answer your support requests.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers. We do not perform automated decision-making that produces legal effects for you.

3. Training policy

We do not train on your prompts or your completions. Not by default, not silently, and not as a condition of any plan. Your inputs and outputs are never used to train, fine-tune, or evaluate Canonn models, and are never sold or shared for anyone else's training.

The only exception would be a separate written agreement that you sign, for your own benefit — for example if you asked us to build a model tuned to your documents. Absent that document, the answer is no.

Our models are trained on public datasets and on data we generate ourselves. Customer traffic plays no part in it.

I/O logging

Prompt and completion bodies are stored only while you have enabled I/O logging on your account. It exists so you can debug your own integration; it is off by default, visible as a switch in the dashboard, and disabling it stops new capture immediately. Bodies are truncated to reasonable size limits before storage.

5. Security and where inference runs

  • All traffic is encrypted in transit with TLS.
  • API keys are stored only as SHA-256 hashes. A key is displayed once at creation and cannot be recovered afterwards — only revoked and replaced.
  • Inference runs on dedicated GPU instances in Finland (EU). Authentication, rate limiting and metering run at the edge on a global CDN network.
  • We serve our own model on our own infrastructure. Your prompts are not forwarded to any third-party model provider.
  • For organizations whose data may not leave their network, a self-hosted deployment is available; in that case no prompt ever reaches us at all.

6. Google user data

The Canonn app can connect your Google Drive using the read-only scope (drive.readonly). It works like this:

  • Only the files you explicitly select are fetched, directly from Google's API to your device, over TLS.
  • Fetched documents are converted to text and indexed locally on your device for retrieval. Google user data is not transferred to or stored on Canonn's servers.
  • Google user data is never used to train models, never sold, and never used for advertising.
  • OAuth tokens are held in a dedicated, SOC 2 Type II audited token vault, and are deleted when you disconnect the source — which you can do at any time from the app.

Canonn's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Processors

We use a small number of vendors to run the service: a cloud edge and database provider, a GPU compute provider (EU), an identity provider for sign-in, a token vault for source connections, and a payment processor. Each processes data only to provide its function, under contract.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, including the GDPR, you may request access to your personal data, correction, deletion, portability, or restriction of processing, and you may object to processing. Write to hello@canonn.ai; we respond within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

You can delete logged prompt and completion bodies yourself at any time by disabling I/O logging and requesting deletion of what was already captured.

9. International transfers

Inference is served from the EU. Some vendors (edge, identity, payments) operate globally; where personal data leaves the EEA we rely on adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses.

10. Children

The service is not directed at children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their data.
